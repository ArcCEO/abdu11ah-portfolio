Your questionsanswered.

What do I need to give you?
A logo, photos of your work, a few reviews, and a list of your services and the areas you cover. I'll send you a simple checklist so nothing gets missed.
How long does it take?
About two weeks once I have your content. Slow replies on content or feedback push the date back, and I'll tell you when that's happening.
Will it get me more calls?
A good website makes it easier for customers to trust you and to contact you. I can't promise rankings or a number of calls, because those also depend on your reviews, your Google profile, your competitors and how quickly you answer. What I do promise is a professional site, a quote form that's tested and working, and fixes for any defects for 14 days after launch.
I already have a website.
We can redo it. You keep your domain and I point it at the new site, so your address doesn't change.
What does it cost to keep it running?
The domain renews yearly, usually $12 to $25. Hosting is set up in your own account and is typically free for a site this size. The care plan is optional.
Can I cancel the care plan?
Yes, with 30 days' notice. The site stays yours, and I'll hand over everything you need to keep it running.
Do you only work with roofers and plumbers?
Mostly, because I know what those customers look for. Other local service businesses are welcome. Book a call and we'll check whether it's a fit.

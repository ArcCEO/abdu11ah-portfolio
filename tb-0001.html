About the project

For Nexus, we're embarking on a comprehensive brand development and digital transformation journey. Our team will craft a cohesive visual identity that embodies Nexus's premium yet accessible ethos in the men's grooming space. This includes designing a versatile logo system, developing a distinctive color palette, and creating a custom typography set that will be applied across all brand touchpoints.

Throughout the project, we'll provide regular consultations, revisions based on feedback, and prepare presentation decks for stakeholder meetings. Our team will be available for ongoing support and training to ensure a smooth handover of all deliverables.

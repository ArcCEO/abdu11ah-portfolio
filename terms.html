1. About Our Services

Abdullah Saliu Design is an independent design studio that helps SaaS companies, startups, and premium businesses create digital products and websites that improve user experience, strengthen brands, and support business growth.

Our services include, but are not limited to:

  • UX/UI Audits

  • Product Design

  • MVP Design

  • Product Redesign

  • Design Systems

  • Landing Page Design

  • Marketing Website Design

  • Business Website Design

  • Website Redesign

  • Design Consulting

  • Ongoing Design Partnerships

Every project is customized to the client's needs.

2. Project Process

Every engagement generally follows the same process:

  1. Discovery Call

  2. Project Proposal

  3. Scope Confirmation

  4. Contract Agreement

  5. Initial Payment

  6. Design Execution

  7. Feedback & Revisions

  8. Final Delivery

Project timelines begin only after all required information, assets, and the agreed payment have been received.

3. Proposals & Scope

Each project includes a written proposal outlining:

  • Project objectives

  • Scope of work

  • Deliverables

  • Timeline

  • Investment

  • Revision limits

  • Payment schedule

Only the work specifically described in the proposal is included.

Requests outside the agreed scope may require a revised timeline and additional fees.

4. Client Responsibilities

To ensure projects move efficiently, clients agree to:

  • Provide accurate project information

  • Supply required content, branding, and assets

  • Respond to feedback requests within a reasonable timeframe

  • Review work promptly

  • Designate a primary decision-maker whenever possible

Delays in communication may affect project timelines.

5. Payments

Payment terms are specified in each proposal.

Unless otherwise agreed:

  • A deposit is required before work begins.

  • Remaining payments are due according to the agreed milestone schedule.

  • Final files are delivered after the final payment has been received.

Late payments may result in paused work until outstanding balances are settled.

6. Revisions

Projects include the number of revision rounds specified in the proposal.

A revision refers to refining or improving an approved direction.

Requests involving significant new features, major redesigns, or changes to previously approved work may be treated as additional scope and quoted separately.

7. Design Partnership

For clients engaging in an ongoing design partnership:

  • Work is planned collaboratively based on agreed priorities.

  • Response times and availability are defined before the engagement begins.

  • Either party may end the partnership with reasonable written notice unless otherwise agreed.

Specific partnership terms are outlined in the individual agreement.

  • Abdullah Saliu Design retains ownership of preliminary concepts, unused explorations, internal working files, design methodologies, and reusable systems unless otherwise agreed.

The client is responsible for ensuring they have the legal right to use any content, images, logos, or trademarks they provide.

  • Project scope changes.

Project deadlines may be adjusted accordingly.

Clients may cancel a project before work begins.
